Hi, I am Colin.

I obtained a Ph.D. in the field of Time Series processing and Machine Learning in late 2020.

My Ph.D. thesis took place at the Orange company, in collaboration with INRIA labs (LACODAM). In the context of infrastructure performances, I used Time Series datasets to forecast the performances of the servers for next day(s) to come. I proposed a Seasonal Time Series framework and several implementations of it.

I am now Head of Data/AI in Orange France CyberSOC.

I talk Python, I am DevOps-compliant, I love CI/CD, I use Docker, I tweak LLMs, I build tested & stable software; often in the cloud.

I am always open to new opportunities, new projects, new challenges. Do not hesitate to contact me!
